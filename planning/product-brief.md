# MACROmate Product Brief

## Problem

Nutrition tracking apps help individuals record what they have eaten or are about to eat, but they can be cumbersome when planning out daily or future intake to meet individual macro targets.

## Solution

MACROmate is a pantry-centered meal-planning application that helps users meet nutrition goals by allowing them to plan daily intake based on their individual nutritional goals. 

Users can save frequently eaten foods, evaluate food quantities against their remaining daily calorie and macro targets, and plan future meals with immediate feedback and guidance. 

## Target User

Individuals who track their macros and want a faster, more convenient way to plan meals around familiar foods.

## Core Value

No more asking, "What have I already eaten?" MACROmate answers the question, "What can I still eat?"

MACROmate prioritizes meal planning over traditional meal tracking. 

## MVP 1.0 Features

### Daily Goals

- Set and update daily calorie and macro targets.
- View planned intake totals, remaining allowances, and track progress toward goals.

### My Pantry

- Save commonly used foods to "My Pantry".
- Calculate Maximum Within Targets per Pantry item and identify limiting factor.
- Search an external nutrition database and select foods to add to the Pantry.
- Preview macro and calorie contributions based on selected quantities.
- Allow users freedom to exceed targets, while providing appropriate guidance and feedback.

### Today's Meals

- Add specified quantities of foods from "My Pantry" into "Today's Meals".
- Edit quantities and remove entries.
- Provide immediate feedback by recalculating daily totals and remaining calorie and macro allowances.
- Clear all entries manually to begin a new planning session. 

### Application Experience

- Persist Pantry items, set goals, and meal entries locally.
- Reset all application data through a confirmed action. 
- Support mobile-first, responsive layouts. 
- Provide confirmation dialogs, keyboard-accessible interactions, and appropriate validation. 





## Out of Scope for MVP

- User authentication and accounts
- Persistent backend database
- Barcode scanning
- AI-generated meal suggestions
- Social media features
- Wearable integrations
- Micronutrient tracking
- Saved meal categories and reusable meals
- Custom saved multi-ingredient recipe items

## Success Criteria

The user can open the app, configure nutritional goals, find or add custom food items into My Pantry, evaluate application guidance on the quantities of each item with respect to how they fit into remaining targets, and assemble a daily meal plan. 

Adding, editing, or removing meal entries immediately updates intake totals and planning guidance. 

MACROmate retains user data between planning sessions, supports core workflow on a variety of devices, and integrates an external nutrition API. 

## Product Direction Beyond MVP

MACROmate's roadmap for potential short-term future enhancements include suggested Pantry item combinations to meet remaining targets, meal grouping, alternate serving units, and more flexible goal configurations.
